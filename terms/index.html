Service & Texting Terms

Effective August 23, 2026

Beta concierge service

Cox Concierge Services is a beta personal-concierge coordination service for participating Kissing Tree households. The selected tier determines the number of included requests available during each monthly period.

Included and à-la-carte work

An included request covers concierge research, direction, setup, or coordination within the scope shown for the customer’s tier. Provider labor, materials, tickets, purchases, transportation, and other third-party charges are not included. No à-la-carte work or charge begins until the customer approves the proposed scope and price.

Text-message program

When you select Text during household setup and check the separate consent box, you agree to receive recurring service-related text messages from Cox Concierge Services at 361-266-4431. Messages may include request confirmations, questions needed to coordinate a request, quote or payment updates, and request status updates. Message frequency varies according to your activity. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply START to resume or HELP for help. You may also contact donnieandbonita@gmail.com or 361-266-4431. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Privacy Policy.

Independent providers

Cox Concierge helps customers locate and coordinate independent service providers. Unless expressly agreed in writing, the customer hires and pays the provider directly. Cox Concierge is not the provider’s employer, insurer, guarantor, or warranty provider.

Safety and emergencies

Cox Concierge is not an emergency, medical, legal, financial-advice, or transportation-carrier service. Do not use the service or text line for emergencies; call 911 or the appropriate emergency service.

Changes and contact

Because this is a beta, features and procedures may change. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Questions may be sent to donnieandbonita@gmail.com.
