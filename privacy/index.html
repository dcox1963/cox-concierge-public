Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026

What we collect

Cox Concierge Services collects information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, mobile phone number, contact preference, service requests, saved ideas, and communications. We may also maintain request status, quote, and payment-status information needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to coordinate the help or activities you request, provide confirmations and status updates, respond to questions, prepare quotes, administer payments, and improve the service.

Mobile and text-message privacy

If you choose text updates, your mobile number and text-message consent are used only for Cox Concierge service communications. Mobile information, text-message opt-in data, and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may use communications providers such as Twilio only to deliver and support these messages.

Service providers and payments

We share only the information reasonably needed to coordinate a request with a provider you authorize. Payment processors such as Stripe process payment details under their own privacy practices; Cox Concierge does not store full card numbers in this public website.

Your choices

You may change your contact preference at any time. Reply STOP to a Cox Concierge text to stop text messages, or HELP for help. You may request correction or deletion of your information, subject to records reasonably needed for transactions, safety, or legal obligations.
